## Replacing the imagery

For a real store, replace the files in `apps/web/public/images/products/` with original product photography, then update `packages/db/prisma/demo-photos.json` or the product image records. Keep the 3:4 aspect ratio for product cards and the 1200×630 social image at `apps/web/public/og-default.png`.
